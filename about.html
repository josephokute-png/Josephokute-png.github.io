About Me

I am a Food Science and Nutrition student at JKUAT with a strong interest in technology and its application in improving health and nutrition systems. I am passionate about using digital tools and data-driven approaches to address real-world challenges in food and public health.

📖 Biography

My name is Joseph Okute, and I am a dedicated student pursuing a professional career in nutrition. I have always been passionate about understanding how food, health, and human biology connect, and this interest naturally led me into nutrition science.

I come from a background where proper nutrition is often overlooked, yet it plays a crucial role in preventing disease and improving quality of life. This motivated me to study nutrition so I can contribute to better health outcomes.

🌟 Early Life

I was born and raised in an environment where community, health, and hard work were highly valued. Growing up, I observed how nutrition affects everyday life, especially where proper dietary knowledge is limited. My early experiences sparked my curiosity about the relationship between diet, health, and human development.

🚀 My Journey

Today, I'm proud to be a Community Voices Network (CVN) member, using my voice and skills to advocate for better nutrition and health awareness in our communities. My journey continues as I combine nutrition science with data analytics to create meaningful impact.
